The wave excerpt comes from [transitking's original Freesound recording](https://www.freesound.org/people/transitking/sounds/11505/). The magic pack comes from [krank](https://sourceforge.net/projects/krank/), whose original project additionally identifies its license as Public Domain.

All sources listed above use **CC0-1.0**: [license summary](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) and [legal text](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode). CC0 permits copying, modification and distribution, including commercial use, without requiring attribution. These credits preserve provenance and acknowledge the creators; they do not imply creator endorsement.

The clips combine recorded animals, water and foley with designed game effects. The rocket uses theMinesAreShakin's low-rumble Rocket Engine effect; **no NASA audio is included**. Lunar landing accents are stylized cues for the classroom animation.

[SOURCES.json](SOURCES.json) records verified source and download URLs, original file sizes and SHA-256 hashes (and, for the authored sets, the generator script), plus the selected-file and editing manifest. Hashes identify the downloaded originals before trimming, mixing or encoding; they are not hashes of the finished reveal tracks.
